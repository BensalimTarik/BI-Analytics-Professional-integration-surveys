# Student Labor Market Insertion Surveys | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard dedicated to analyzing the professional integration of students.

It provides insights into employment outcomes, company integration, geographical distribution, professional experience, working conditions, job sectors and salary indicators.

The dashboard covers the academic years **2022–2023** and **2023–2024**, with interactive filters allowing users to explore results by division, school, campus and training period.

## Main Analytical Areas

- **Employment Overview:** response rates and employment outcomes.
- **Division, Campus and School Analysis:** comparison of employment indicators across organizational units.
- **Geographical Distribution:** employment by city and region.
- **Company Integration:** company-level employment rates and leading recruiting organizations.
- **Professional Experience:** experience levels and years of experience.
- **Working Conditions:** contract types and work arrangements.
- **Job Sectors and Occupations:** analysis of employment sectors, sub-sectors and occupations.
- **Salary and Unemployment:** average salary and unemployment period by employment sector.

## Interactive Filters

- Division
- School name
- Region / Campus
- Training period (2022–2023 and 2023–2024)
